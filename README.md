# 🏀 NBA Fantasy League: Trade Eligibility Rules

> **The Core Policy**: Active managers stay trade-eligible. If you neglect your roster, your trading privileges are frozen until active management is restored.

---

## ⚡ The 4 Rules in Plain English

* **Rule 1: Start Active Players (No Wasted Slots)**  
  Start players who have games. Don't leave starter spots empty or bench active players playing tonight while an inactive or off-day player sits in your starting lineup.

* **Rule 2: Use Your IR Slot**  
  Move sidelined `OUT` players into your single IR spot instead of letting them sit on your active or bench roster while your IR spot sits empty.

* **Rule 3: Drop Season-Ending Injuries**  
  Drop players who are officially ruled out for the remainder of the NBA season. No stashing dead roster spots.

* **Rule 4: Maintain a Legal Roster Size**  
  Keep at least 13 total players on your roster and never hold more than 1 player in your IR slot.

---

## ⏱️ How Freezes & Clearance Work

### Rule 1: The 14-Game-Day Rolling Window
* **Trade Freeze**: If you waste a starting slot on **4 or more days** across the last 14 NBA game days, your ability to trade freezes immediately.
* **How to Unfreeze**: Your wasted days inside the trailing 14-day window must drop down to **1 or 0**. 
* **No Quick-Fix Trick**: You cannot neglect your team for weeks, set your lineup for a single day to force a trade through, and walk away. Older violations must naturally roll off the back of the 14-day window through consistent, active management.

### Rules 2, 3, & 4: 2-Day Grace Period
* **Grace Period**: You have **2 game days** to fix an unused IR slot, drop a season-ending injury, or correct your roster size.
* **Trade Freeze**: Trades freeze on the **3rd consecutive day** of neglect.
* **Clearing**: Clears after **2 consecutive clean game days**.

## 📖 Quick Example: How Rule 1 Works (Team No Cap)

1. **Days 1–9 (Falling Ineligible)**: No Cap leaves an active bench player who plays on the bench on Days 2, 4, 6, and 9 while starting players who have no game or are OUT.  
   $\rightarrow$ **Result**: Reaches **4 wasted days** in the 14-day window $\rightarrow$ **TRADES FROZEN**.
2. **Day 10 (The 1-Day Fix Fails)**: No Cap sets a perfect lineup.  
   $\rightarrow$ **Result**: Still **FROZEN**. Looking back over the last 14 days, Days 2, 4, 6, and 9 are still in the window (Count: 4).
3. **Days 15–20 (Earning It Back)**: As new game days are played, older violations roll out of the 14-day window:
   - *Day 16*: Day 2 rolls off (3 violations left $\rightarrow$ still frozen).
   - *Day 18*: Day 4 rolls off (2 violations left $\rightarrow$ still frozen).
   - *Day 20*: Day 6 rolls off (Only 1 violation left) $\rightarrow$ **🎉 TRADES UNFROZEN**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

* **What if all 10 of my starters have games tonight?**  
  You are 100% fine. Leaving extra bench players who also play on the bench is expected—you can only start 10 players.
* **What if my entire bench is injured or has the night off?**  
  Zero penalty. You can't play someone who doesn't have a game.
* **What if my bench player can't legally play the open position?**  
  Zero penalty. If your open spot is Center and your only bench player is a Point Guard, no violation occurs.
* **What about Questionable or Day-to-Day players?**  
  Legal to start. Only players confirmed **`OUT`** or with **`NO GAME`** count as open spots.
