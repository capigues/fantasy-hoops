# NBA Fantasy League: Trade Eligibility Rules

Active managers stay trade-eligible. If a roster is neglected, trading privileges are frozen until active management is restored.

---

## The 4 Rules

* **Rule 1: Start Active Players (No Wasted Slots)**  
  Start players who have games. Don't leave starter spots empty or bench active players playing tonight while an inactive or off-day player sits in your starting lineup.

* **Rule 2: Use Your IR Slot**  
  Move sidelined `OUT` players into your single IR spot instead of letting them sit on your active or bench roster while your IR spot sits empty.

* **Rule 3: Drop Season-Ending Injuries**  
  Drop players who are officially ruled out for the remainder of the NBA season. No stashing dead roster spots.

* **Rule 4: Maintain a Complete Roster**  
  Keep at least 13 total players on your roster. Dropping players without replacement creates an illegal roster.

---

## Freezes & Clearance

### Rule 1: 14-Game-Day Window
* **Trade Freeze**: Wasting a starting slot on **4 or more days** in the last 14 NBA game days freezes your ability to trade.
* **Clearing**: Wasted days inside the 14-day window must drop to **1 or 0**. Older violations roll out of the window as new clean game days are played. Setting a lineup for one day will not clear an existing freeze.

### Rules 2, 3, & 4: 2-Day Grace Period
* **Grace Period**: You have **2 game days** to move a player to IR, drop an out-for-season player, or restore your roster size.
* **Trade Freeze**: Trades freeze on the **3rd consecutive game day** of an unresolved violation.
* **Clearing**: Clears after **2 consecutive clean game days**.

---

## Rule 1 Walkthrough (Team No Cap)

* **Days 1–9**: No Cap leaves an active bench player who plays on the bench on Days 2, 4, 6, and 9 while starting players with no game or OUT. Reaches **4 wasted days** in the 14-day window → **Trades Frozen**.
* **Day 10**: No Cap sets a full lineup. Trades remain **Frozen** because Days 2, 4, 6, and 9 are still within the trailing 14-day window.
* **Days 15–20**: As new clean game days are played, older violations roll out of the window:
  - *Day 16*: Day 2 rolls off (3 violations remain → still frozen).
  - *Day 18*: Day 4 rolls off (2 violations remain → still frozen).
  - *Day 20*: Day 6 rolls off (1 violation remains) → **Trades Unfrozen**.

---

## FAQ

* **What if all 10 of my starters have games tonight?**  
  Leaving extra bench players who also play on the bench is expected and valid. You can only start 10 players.
* **What if my entire bench is injured or has the night off?**  
  No penalty. You cannot replace players if nobody on your bench has a game.
* **What if my bench player cannot play the open slot?**  
  No penalty. If your open slot is Center and your only bench player is a Point Guard, you are not penalized.
* **What about Questionable or Day-to-Day players?**  
  Legal to start. Only players confirmed **`OUT`** or with **`NO GAME`** count as open spots.
